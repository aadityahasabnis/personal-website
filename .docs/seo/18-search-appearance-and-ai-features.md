# Search Appearance and AI Features Guide

## Purpose

Provide practical guidance for how content appears in Google Search surfaces, including AI Overviews and AI Mode.

## Core Principle

There are no special technical optimizations required solely for AI Overviews or AI Mode beyond solid SEO fundamentals.

## Eligibility Basics

To appear as supporting links in AI features, content generally must:

- Be indexable and indexed in Google Search
- Be eligible for snippet display
- Meet Search technical requirements and policies

## What Still Matters

The same fundamentals continue to drive outcomes:

- Crawlability and indexability
- Helpful, people-first content
- Strong internal linking
- High quality page experience
- Clear, visible text content
- Accurate structured data aligned to visible content

## Controls for AI Feature Exposure

Use existing snippet controls when needed:

- `nosnippet`
- `data-nosnippet`
- `max-snippet`
- `noindex`

Important:

- These controls work only when Google can crawl the page.

## Measurement Guidance

Traffic from AI features is included in Search Console web performance data.

Recommended measurement stack:

1. Search Console performance trend analysis
2. Analytics conversion quality checks
3. Landing-page engagement and retention review

## Project Implementation Notes

- Keep all public SEO routes crawlable and snippet-eligible unless intentionally restricted.
- Keep schema, metadata, and visible content consistent.
- Treat AI-feature traffic as part of holistic Search optimization, not a separate stack.
