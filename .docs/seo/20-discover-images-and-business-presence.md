# Discover, Image SEO, and Business Presence Guide

## Purpose

Capture implementation rules for Discover visibility, image SEO quality, and business identity in Search.

## Discover Readiness

### Eligibility

- Indexed content that meets Discover policies is automatically eligible
- No special tags or structured data are required just for Discover

### Strong practices

- Clear non-clickbait headlines
- Timely or uniquely insightful content
- Large, compelling images
- Strong page experience
- `max-image-preview:large` where large previews are desired

## Image SEO Standards

### Discovery and indexing

- Use crawlable HTML image elements (`img` with `src`)
- Avoid relying on CSS background images for primary indexed visuals
- Use image sitemap when discovery coverage needs improvement

### Quality and metadata

- Provide high quality images
- Use descriptive filenames
- Provide useful alt text
- Specify preferred image using `og:image` and/or structured data image fields

### Formats and responsiveness

- Use supported formats (such as JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, SVG)
- Implement responsive image patterns with robust fallback `src`

## Business Presence in Search

### Foundations

- Verify website ownership in Search Console
- Keep business profile information current when applicable
- Maintain organization and breadcrumb structured data where relevant

### Practical outcomes

- Stronger branded appearance
- Better recognition of official site identity
- More reliable local/business detail presentation where applicable

## Project Implementation Notes

- Ensure social preview and schema image references are consistent and crawlable.
- Continue to maintain author/site identity graph for brand clarity.
